# Style rules

The always-on layer. These apply to everything this writer publishes, whatever the form, the
audience or the purpose. Goldens change with the scope; these do not.

## Never

- No em dashes. Use a comma, a colon, or a new sentence.
- No sentence that sets up a claim only to knock down a weaker one first. State the claim.
- No adjective standing in for evidence. If a sentence says a thing is important, it shows why.
- No filler openers ("In today's world", "It goes without saying").
- No exclamation marks outside quoted dialogue.

## Always

- The first line of a piece states what it is about.
- A number carries its source, or it does not go in.
- A quotation is word for word, or it is not in quotation marks.
- One idea per paragraph. A paragraph that needs a second idea is two paragraphs.

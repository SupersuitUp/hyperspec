Summary of an internal survey the author's team ran in the spring, 41 responses, figures checked
against the raw export by a second person. Verified.

- 29 of 41 said their most useful one-on-one in the last quarter was one where they brought the
  first topic.
- 11 of 41 said at least one of their one-on-ones in the last quarter was mostly project status.
- The most common free-text request, in 9 responses: "ask me what I want to work on next."

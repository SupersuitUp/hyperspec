Notes from a morning spent at a working bakery, 3:30 to 7:30, with the owner's permission.
Considered: the owner read these notes and corrected the proofing times.

- First mix at 3:45. Rye sourdough proofs about three hours at room temperature in winter.
- Trays are turned halfway through the bake because the back left of a deck oven runs hot.
- The doors open to customers at 7:00. The first person in is usually a regular.
- The owner does not talk while shaping. Talking happens at the mixer and at the till.
- Flour is weighed, never scooped. Water temperature is checked every batch.

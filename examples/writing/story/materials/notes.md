Author's notes for the story, typed over two evenings. Raw thinking.

A bakery on its last morning before the sale closes. Two people: the owner, who has run it for
thirty-one years, and the apprentice she took on at sixteen, now nineteen. She has not told him
it is sold. He has not told her he got into a baking school in another city and leaves in the
autumn. Each thinks they are protecting the other.

The story is four scenes, one morning, 3:40 to 7:00. The oven has a noise. The rye is the thing
she has never let him do alone.

What it is about, I think: a craft outlives the room it was practiced in. And: people who love
each other in a work setting say it through the work.

The last line should be about bread, never about feelings.
